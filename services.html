What the engagement includes

1. Intake and acceptance. We review stack, severity, reproduction quality, confidentiality, and browser targets.
2. Evidence capture. FAULTLINE/Playwright capture is used where appropriate; credentials and private production secrets are never requested through the public form.
3. Deterministic reduction. The failing case is minimized while preserving the oracle and causal evidence.
4. Verification. The reduced case is checked against agreed Chromium, Firefox, WebKit/Safari, CI or GitHub Action targets.
5. Handoff. You receive the reproducer, evidence, findings, recommended fix direction, and integration notes.
