# Code of Conduct

FAULTLINE is an open debugging/tooling community. Participation is welcome regardless of experience level, background or employer, provided discussion remains technically rigorous and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code, reproductions and evidence rather than people.
- Distinguish measured reducer behavior from assumptions or stronger minimality claims.
- Help first-time contributors turn browser bugs into small synthetic cases.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's source, credentials or security-sensitive data without authorization.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when recommending tools or benchmarks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, spam, fabricated benchmark/reduction evidence, deceptive contribution activity, public disclosure of unpatched exploit details without coordination and attempts to manipulate stars/votes/contributor counts are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject or remove comments, issues, pull requests or other contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Security-sensitive findings must follow `SECURITY.md`. For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
